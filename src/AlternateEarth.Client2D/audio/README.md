Exact original names and download/source URLs are recorded in `sources.json`. Files are served locally; gameplay never contacts these sites.

Horns, engines, the cartoon UFO motor, weapons, explosions, pouring, scissors, and additional creature sounds are original procedural audio generated by `../game-audio.js`. Those generated audio samples are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The UFO is an original warbling cartoon motor, not a recording from The Jetsons.

CC0 legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The mix uses a fixed 55% master gain; listening volume is controlled by the computer. There are no in-game volume or mute controls. Legacy saved volume and mute settings are ignored. Audio begins after a click/key press, fades with distance, stays within the player's location, and silences when the tab is hidden or disconnected. Motors are limited to the six closest audible vehicles. Delayed impacts are discarded when the tab is hidden or the location changes.

Gas engines idle at a low pitch and smoothly rev with measured speed, with a quiet 1.8–4% source gain (8–11% for the bus diesel, with stronger speaker-friendly harmonics) before master volume and distance attenuation. Fire crackle follows active burns (up to four nearby fires). Flamethrower whooshes, low projectile thumps, fart effects, T-Rex roars, herbivore bellows and raptor rasps are also original synthesized CC0 audio.
